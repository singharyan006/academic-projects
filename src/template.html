    Hardware architecture

    Wiring of the physical prototype. Raw battery voltage (orange) only feeds the regulator and the Arduino VIN; the servos run from the regulated 5 V rail (red). All grounds are common.

    ⚡ Never connect the raw 2S battery (7.4 V) directly to the servo rail. Always use a 5 V buck regulator/UBEC rated ≥ 3 A.

    Power notes

    • 2S Li-ion/LiPo: 7.4 V nominal, ~8.4 V fully charged, ~6.0–6.4 V when flat.
    • SG90s are 4.8–6 V parts. A raw 2S pack on the servo rail over-volts them. It must go through a 5 V buck/UBEC first.
    • Size the regulator for ~0.5–0.7 A per SG90 under load or stall: 4 servos → 2.5–3 A minimum.
    • Do not power the servos from the UNO 5V pin or USB. Simultaneous moves cause brown-outs and resets.
    • Put a 470–1000 µF electrolytic across the servo rail near the servos.
    • Servo GND, regulator GND and Arduino GND must be joined, or the PWM signals have no reference.

    Firmware ↔ simulation mapping

    • Servo.write() targets ⇄ motion engine targets (enable “servo commands” in the log to see each command).
    • tcs.getRawData(&r,&g,&b,&c) ⇄ simulated TCS34725 model (gain, ambient, noise).
    • Classifier thresholds ⇄ identical rule order and values (Calibration tab).
    • Sorting switch(state) ⇄ 15-state machine shown in the Simulation tab.
    No serial link is implemented. This page never talks to an Arduino. The simulation core exposes a servo-command event stream so a Web Serial bridge could be added later in a non-sandboxed build.
